MATH survey of algebra - wk 4 groups!!

def: a group is a set G w/ an operation * s.t.
1) closure: a*b in G for all a,b in G
2) associative: (a*b)*c = a*(b*c)
3) identity: exists e w/ e*a = a*e = a
4) inverses: every a has a^-1 w/ a*a^-1 = e
(prof: "CAII" to remember?? closure assoc identity inverse)

examples
- Z under + : yes. identity 0, inverse of a is -a
- Z under x : NO!! 2 has no inverse in Z (1/2 not integer)
- Q\{0} under x : yes
- R under + : yes
- Z_n under addition mod n : yes. e.g Z_5 = {0,1,2,3,4}

abelian = commutative, a*b = b*a
Z, Z_n are abelian
matrices under mult are NOT abelian (AB != BA usually)
GL(2,R) = invertible 2x2 matrices, non abelian

order of group |G| = # elements
order of element a = smallest n>0 w/ a^n = e
ex in Z_6: order of 2 is 3 bc 2+2+2 = 6 = 0

cyclic group = generated by one element, G = <a>
Z_n is cyclic, generated by 1
every cyclic group is abelian (not other way round!)

subgroup H of G: subset thats also a group w/ same operation
subgroup test: nonempty + a*b^-1 in H for all a,b in H

lagranges thm: if H subgroup of finite G, then |H| divides |G|
-> so group of order 7 only has subgroups of order 1 and 7
corollary: order of any element divides |G|
