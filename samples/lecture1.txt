diff from classical: classical = involuntary responses (drool, fear)
operant = VOLUNTARY behaviour + its consequences

thorndike first - cats in puzzle boxes, got faster at escaping over trials
"law of effect" -> behaviours w/ good outcome get stamped in, bad outcome stamped out

skinner - skinner box (operant chamber). rat presses lever -> food pellet
pigeons too, pecking a key

the 4 combos (prof said this WILL be on exam!!)
pos = ADD something, neg = REMOVE something (not good/bad!!)
reinforcement = behaviour goes UP, punishment = behaviour goes DOWN
- pos reinforcement: add good thing -> more behaviour (treat for dog sitting)
- neg reinforcement: take away bad thing -> more behaviour (seatbelt beeping stops when u buckle)
- pos punishment: add bad thing -> less behaviour (speeding ticket)
- neg punishment: take away good thing -> less behaviour (phone taken away)
ppl confuse neg reinforcment w/ punishment ALL the time

schedules of reinforcemnt
continuous = every time, fast learning but fast extinction
partial:
FR fixed ratio - every nth response (coffee card, 10th free)
VR variable ratio - random # of responses -> slot machines!! most resistant to extinction
FI fixed interval - first response after set time (paycheque). scalloped pattern
VI variable interval - random time (checking email/texts)

shaping = reinforce successive approximations. how u teach a rat to do complex stuff
or teaching a kid to tie shoes step by step

primary reinforcer = food, water (biological)
secondary = money, grades, praise (learned value)

real life ex: my phone apps w/ streaks lol, variable rewards keep u checking
