BIOL - cellular respiration (ch 9)

big picture: glucose + O2 -> CO2 + H2O + ATP
C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy
its a redox rxn, glucose oxidized, O2 reduced

3 (or 4??) stages - prof counts pyruvate oxidation seperate
1. glycolysis
- in CYTOPLASM, doesnt need O2 (anaerobic)
- glucose (6C) -> 2 pyruvate (3C)
- uses 2 ATP, makes 4 -> NET 2 ATP
- also 2 NADH
2. pyruvate oxidation
- pyruvate goes into mitochondria
- -> acetyl CoA, releases CO2, makes NADH
3. krebs cycle / citric acid cycle
- mitochondrial MATRIX
- 2 turns per glucose (bc 2 pyruvate!!)
- makes NADH, FADH2, CO2, 2 ATP total
4. ETC + oxidative phosphorylation
- INNER mitochondrial membrane (cristae)
- NADH + FADH2 drop off electrons
- pumps H+ ions -> gradient -> ATP synthase makes ATP (chemiosmosis)
- O2 = FINAL electron acceptor, forms water
- makes MOST ATP ~26-28

total ~30-32 ATP per glucose (older books say 36-38, prof said either ok if explained)

no oxygen?? -> fermentation
- lactic acid fermentation: muscles during sprint, the burn
- alcoholic fermentation: yeast -> ethanol + CO2 (bread, beer)
- only gets 2 ATP from glycolysis, regenerates NAD+ so glycolysis can keep going

mitochondria = "powerhouse of the cell" (yes she actually said it)
cyanide blocks the ETC -> why its deadly
